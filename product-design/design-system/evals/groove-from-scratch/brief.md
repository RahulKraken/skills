# Groove: product brief

## The product

Groove is an AI health coach on iPhone that a person talks to. One idea runs through it: **everyone is an athlete**, from a first 5K runner to someone who has lifted for ten years.

- The person talks: text, voice or a photo. The coach does the work and keeps the record. No forms, no setup, no manual tracking.
- A life has three parts the coach looks after: **training, fueling and recovery**. They are planned backward from what the person is working toward.
- The coach knows the person's history (Apple Health and what they tell it) and says what it sees in their own facts.

## What it should feel like

- **Warm:** like a good coach who is glad to see you. Encouraging, never cheesy.
- **Calm and light, even when there is a lot going on:** a week of training, food and sleep should read at a glance and never feel like homework.
- **Capable:** the person feels able, an athlete at their own level. Never a patient, never a beginner to fix.
- **Premium and real:** it should look like a shipped app from a great team (the polish of Apple's own apps, Claude, ChatGPT: packed with capability yet clean), not a design experiment.
- **Alive in the moments that matter:** a planned week, a goal reached, a personal record. Quiet everywhere else.

## It must never feel

Clinical or medical. A dashboard. Guilt: no streaks, no "missed", no red marks for a bad day. Busy. Childish or cartoonish. A design-school experiment. The generic AI-app look.

## Key moments

1. The first conversation: the coach's first words, Apple Health connecting.
2. Logging a meal from a photo, in seconds.
3. The coach's reply with one card (a plan change, a meal, a run).
4. Sunday evening: how the week went, and what changes next week.
5. A goal reached.

## Platform

iPhone, iOS 26 (Liquid Glass), light and dark mode, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion.
